# Harim Principles

> "Knowledge, Support, Security"

These principles guide every decision made in the Harim project.

---

## 1. Evidence Before Opinion

Every educational content must be based on reliable, verifiable and up-to-date sources.

---

## 2. Victim-Centered

Victims are never blamed.

Harim exists to educate, support and empower people, not to judge them.

---

## 3. Privacy First

Privacy is a fundamental right.

Harim collects the minimum amount of information necessary and respects users' confidentiality.

---

## 4. Transparency

We are honest about what we know and what we don't.

If information cannot be verified, it will not be presented as fact.

---

## 5. Open Collaboration

Knowledge grows through collaboration.

Harim welcomes respectful contributions from developers, researchers, translators and educators.

---

## 6. Education Before Technology

Technology is a tool.

Education is the mission.

---

## 7. Respect

Every contributor and every user deserves respect.

Harassment, discrimination and victim blaming have no place in this project.
